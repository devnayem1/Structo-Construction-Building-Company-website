Johnathan Steele

Chief Executive Officer

Johnathan founded Structo in 2009 after fifteen years running commercial sites for one of the largest contractors in the country. He still walks every major project at least once a month, and he still reads every client complaint himself.

His view of construction is simple: a building is a promise made in concrete. If the programme says March, the keys are handed over in March. That standard is what the rest of the company is built around.

  • Experience: 25 years in commercial and residential construction
  • Specialization: Design and build delivery, contract strategy
