Questions About Cost

Is the quoted price fixed?

The Design & Build price is fixed against an agreed specification. It only changes if you ask for something that was not in that specification, and any change is priced and signed off in writing before the work starts.

What does the deposit schedule look like?

Ten percent on contract signing, then staged payments tied to completed milestones rather than calendar dates. You never pay for work that has not been inspected and signed off.

Do the packages include VAT and permits?

Permit and planning fees are listed separately at cost, with no markup. Tax treatment depends on whether the work is new build or a renovation, and we set that out clearly on the quote.
